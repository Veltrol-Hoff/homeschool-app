"This is a native Android application built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Room Database."

"The app is an educational planner designed specifically to manage records for 2 children."

"It must track 875 hours of instruction across 6 mandatory subjects (Reading, Language Arts, Math, Social Studies, Science, Health) to comply with Wisconsin state law."

"The primary user experience relies on 'reverse planning'—logging spontaneous, completed activities rather than strictly scheduling them in advance."
